Telemetry Layer

Live Telemetry Pipeline

The collector samples battery signals, writes them to SQLite, and exposes live readings through FastAPI. This page connects to the running service and plots the current session.

Live Metrics

Voltage
--
Bus voltage from the sensor.
Current
--
Charge or discharge current.
Power
--
Voltage multiplied by current.
Temperature
--
Thermal safety context.
